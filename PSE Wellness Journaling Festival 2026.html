About the event

Step into a day dedicated to creativity, reflection, and self-discovery. The Journaling Festival is a unique journaling event that celebrates the power of writing as a tool for personal growth and wellness. Through inspiring talks held by professional speakers and practical workshops led by educated coaches, attendees will explore the diverse enriching forms of journaling. These include Therapeutic Journaling, Art Journaling, Expressive Journaling and other forms of journal writing.
